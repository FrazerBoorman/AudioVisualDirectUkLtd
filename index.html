Frequently asked questions

Do we need a projector or a large TV?

It depends on viewing distance, room size, ambient light and what you’re showing. In some spaces a high-quality TV is better than a small projector screen; in others, projection is the only way to achieve the right image size.

What is a hearing loop and do we need one?

A hearing loop transmits sound directly to hearing aids set to the “T” (telecoil) setting. If you host public meetings, services or events, a loop is often expected and may be required by your policies.

What happens during a free survey?

We visit your venue, listen to what you’re trying to achieve, look at any existing system, and then suggest options – all without any obligation.

Planning checklists

Use these prompts when discussing AV at committee or board level:

  • Who needs to hear and see clearly, and from where?
  • Which rooms are used most often, and for what?
  • Who will operate the system – and how confident are they with technology?
  • Are there any accessibility, safeguarding or compliance requirements?
  • What time of day is the room used (how bright is it)?
